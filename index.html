ARGOS // ETHICAL INFILTRATION
OPERATION UMBRAL · Ethical Reconfiguration Protocol
What is this activity?

Your team is an operative cell that must infiltrate ARGOS, an educational AI system operating in vulnerable communities without ethical oversight. Your mission: navigate 9 security layers, make critical decisions, and inject an ethical virus that reconfigures the system from within.

⚠ This is not an individual activity. Every decision represents your group's position. Discuss before answering.
Game screen — two panels
◧ Left panel — Intel / Context
This is where the key information for each layer appears: the conceptual framework (what you need to know) and the specific situation your team faces. Read it before answering.
◨ Right panel — Operations / Decision
This is where the question or dilemma and the answer options appear. Only one option can be selected. When the team agrees, press Execute and Advance.
Types of questions
KNOWLEDGE STANCE BIFURCATION
  • Knowledge: these have a correct answer. They assess understanding of key concepts (algorithmic bias, informed consent, legal frameworks, etc.).
  • Stance: there is no right or wrong answer. These are designed to surface and discuss your organization's position on real dilemmas. All options are valid.
  • Bifurcation (Layer 4): you choose the path your organization will follow for the next 4 layers — Technical Governance or Community and Territory.
ARGOS Countermeasures

During the game, ARGOS will trigger security alerts: timed questions that appear unexpectedly. They do not stop the game, but answering them correctly builds your analytical precision score. If time runs out, ARGOS blocks the attempt.

End results

After completing all 9 layers, you will receive an Infiltration Report with: the ethical virus injected (the 9 key words from your decisions), your organization's Agent Profile, recommended frameworks, and the option to download everything as a PDF.
